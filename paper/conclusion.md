
﻿# Conclusion

KECRL provides a structured design for continual reinforcement learning in
which environment mechanism knowledge, executable skills, and task-level
coordination remain distinct. The current controlled CUDA stage validates the
minimal implementation path: Knowledge Evolution can update atomic mechanism
states, qualified Modules can be requested and executed, and Knowledge
Evidence is kept separate from Skill Feedback.

The controlled results are candidate-stage evidence only. They use a
deterministic resource environment, five seeds, a stage Policy backend, and a
diagnostic bootstrap analysis. They do not establish a final performance
advantage or external-environment generalization. Crafter validation, final
statistical inference, and a fully frozen formal backend remain future work.
